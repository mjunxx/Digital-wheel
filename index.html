💭 Short Reflection

Which scientific contribution or intellectual revolution do you think had the greatest impact on how humans understand the world?

I think the Darwinian Revolution had the greatest impact. Before Darwin, most people believed humans stood apart from and above nature, placed in a fixed and unchanging order. The theory of evolution by natural selection showed instead that humans are part of a long, ongoing process shared with every other living thing, connected by common ancestry rather than separated from it. This idea reshaped biology, medicine, and even how people think about ethics, race, and human identity. It also opened the door for later revolutions — Freud's view of the unconscious mind, for example, built on the idea that humans are natural beings shaped by biological drives rather than purely rational souls. Because it changed not just one field but the whole framework for understanding life and our place within it, I see the Darwinian Revolution as the most far-reaching turning point on this wheel.
